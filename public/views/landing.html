Unlimited number of conference rooms, no time limit, ever

VCPeer is built radically differently. There's no bulky server in the middle — just peer-to-peer calling, straight from your browser, for the lowest latency and the clearest video.

Screen Sharing

Share your screen, application window, present your documents, slides and more. Up to 8k resolution and speed up to 60fps.

WebCam Streaming

Having the webcam on, allows participants to make a deeper connection with you. Up to 8k resolution and speed up to 60fps.

Chat Room

Chat with others in the meeting, either public chat or private, save the meeting messages. An integrated emoji picker to show your feeling.

Meeting Recording

Record your Screen, WebCam and Audio. Save it for use in the future or to share with others.

Interactive Whiteboard

Advanced collaborative whiteboard to draw and explain your concepts to the other participants in the meeting.

File Sharing

Share any types of files to all participants in the meeting, in total security, thanks to Datagram Transport Layer Security (DTLS).

Virtual Background

Blur your surroundings or replace them with an image or a custom background, to keep your focus and your privacy during the meeting.

ChatGPT

ChatGPT for answering questions, providing information, and connecting users to relevant resources.

Rest API

Seamlessly integrate and power your applications with ease. Quick setup and top-tier support.

Total Privacy

Data stays between you and your participants. VCPeer doesn't collect or share personal information. It's built for privacy first.

Maximum Security

End-to-end state-of-the-art encryption means your participants are exactly that. Your calls.

Access Control

Host protection, OIDC login, JWT credentials and room passwords to decide exactly who can join and manage your meetings.
