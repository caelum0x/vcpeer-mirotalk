Free group video calls for small groups.

Start a free call and share the link with your group. No signup, no download, no time limit on the call.

Honest caveat: VCPeer is peer-to-peer — every participant connects directly to every other participant, with no server relaying the call. That makes it fast and private, and it works best for small groups, up to roughly 5-6 people. For larger calls, quality will depend on each participant's connection and device.

Screen sharing, chat, and a shared whiteboard are built in, free, for every call.
