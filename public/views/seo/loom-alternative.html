Looking for a Loom alternative? Record once, share a link.

Start a free VCPeer call — it works alone, you don't need anyone else on the line — click record, show your screen or your face, and stop whenever you're done. A Get shareable link button appears next to your recording and uploads it in the background.

Send the resulting /watch/<id> link to whoever needs to see it. They open it in a browser, no account, and watch it on their own time — VCPeer counts the views so you know it was actually watched.

Honest caveat: this is the free, no-signup core of "record once, send a link" — up to 1 GiB per recording, links expire after 30 days by default. There's no auto-transcription, comments, or team library (yet). If that's all you need to replace a status update or a bug-report video, it's free and there's nothing to install.
